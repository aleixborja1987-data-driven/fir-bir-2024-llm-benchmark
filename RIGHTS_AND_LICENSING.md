# Rights and licensing — author decision required

The package preserves materials supplied by the corresponding author for review and reproducibility. Its preparation does not establish or transfer redistribution rights. No MIT/CC licence has been assigned on behalf of the authors without their approval.

Examination questions, booklets, extracted images and model outputs may involve third-party rights/terms. Question text also appears within raw prompts and responses; excluding PDFs alone would not remove it. Authors should determine appropriate permissions and any required redactions/restrictions before a public release. Keep scientific identifiers and audit trails traceable if content is restricted.

Select and document a licence for author-owned analysis code and, separately, an appropriate licence for derived data. Record any third-party exceptions. Replace this notice with approved licence files only after authorization. Source material is not represented as public domain merely because it was available online.
